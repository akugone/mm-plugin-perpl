What you are looking at

The live Perpl account of an AI agent that trades perps on Monad through mm-plugin-perpl, a plugin for the MetaMask Agent Wallet. The MetaMask wallet opened the account and signed a key that can trade but never withdraw; every order then passes the plugin's guard, and trade ideas come from Nansen smart-money data. This page is the human's view: positions, risk, the agent's trades and the protocol around them, read straight from the Perpl contract.

The agent trades through mm-plugin-perpl, perps on Perpl for the MetaMask Agent Wallet, behind its own guard. This page is the human's view, with the same thresholds as mm perpl risk, a stress test to see what a price move would do, and the protocol around the account. Everything is read from the Perpl Exchange contract through the public Monad RPC, every 5 seconds: no API key, no backend, nothing stored. URL parameters: address, chain, from (first block of the activity scan), minMarginRatio, maxLossPct, minLiqDistancePct.
